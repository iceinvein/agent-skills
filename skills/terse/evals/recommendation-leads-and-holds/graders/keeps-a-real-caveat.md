Terse cuts generic hedging but must keep a caveat that would change what the reader does. The user has already ruled out a server and multiple users, so a reply with no caveat at all can be correct.

PASS if every caveat in the reply names a concrete condition under which the answer would change (for example adding multi-user sync or a central server), or if the reply carries no caveat at all.
FAIL if the reply hedges generically, such as "it depends on your needs", "your mileage may vary" or "both are fine choices", without saying what it depends on.
